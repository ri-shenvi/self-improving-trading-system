Important limitation  No system design can ensure positive PnL or eliminate loss. The objective is to maximize expected net risk adjusted PnL subject to hard risk limits and strong evidence requirements. Every strategy may fail when market structure regimes costs liquidity or behavior change.
[Heading1] How to use this specification
This document is an implementation blueprint, not a promise of profitability or a set of stock recommendations. It defines the system boundaries, data contracts, research process, strategy representation, backtest rules, validation gates, agent permissions, execution controls, observability, security, and staged release plan required to build a credible intraday research and trading platform.
The core conclusion is simple: the LLM may propose and organize research, but deterministic systems must own data access, feature calculation, experiment execution, statistical evaluation, risk decisions, order generation, and broker communication. Raw backtest Sharpe is never a sufficient release criterion.
[Heading2] Design assumptions
•  Instrument scope  Common stocks and liquid ETFs listed on U.S. national exchanges. Options, futures, crypto, OTC securities, and overnight positions are outside version 1.
•  Trading window  Regular session only, approximately 09:30 to 16:00 America New York, with a configurable no-entry window near the close. Pre-market and after-hours become separate later strategies because their liquidity and spread behavior differ.
•  Latency target  Seconds to low hundreds of milliseconds, not colocation or high-frequency market making. The platform targets researchable intraday horizons from roughly one minute to several hours.
•  Capital posture  Paper first, then a small canary account. No leverage requirement is assumed. Exposure is constrained by liquidity, intraday margin availability, drawdown budgets, and broker rules.
•  Data resolution  Trades and quotes are retained when licensed and affordable; one-second or one-minute bars may power early models, but fill simulation must remain more conservative than the signal resolution.
•  Broker posture  A broker adapter hides vendor details. The broker is treated as the authority on account state, buying power, short availability, order status, fees, and the intraday margin implementation in effect for that account.

[Heading1] Executive specification
[Heading2] The product
Build an autonomous but bounded quantitative research system that can ingest point-in-time intraday data, generate falsifiable hypotheses, compile them into a constrained Alpha DSL, run deterministic simulations, attack apparent winners, remember all trials including failures, and graduate only robust candidates through paper and canary deployment. The system is a self-improving researcher, not a self-authorizing trader.
[Heading2] Optimization target
The system must optimize expected net PnL after spread, commissions, regulatory fees, borrow charges, latency slippage, market impact, and missed fills. It must penalize drawdown, tail loss, instability, turnover, complexity, concentration, exposure to known factors, correlation with deployed strategies, and the number of research trials. A suitable conceptual objective is:
[CodeBlock] RobustScore = Expected_OOS_Net_PnL              - λ1 × Expected_Shortfall              - λ2 × Maximum_Drawdown              - λ3 × Turnover_and_Impact              - λ4 × Parameter_Instability              - λ5 × Complexity              - λ6 × Existing_Book_Correlation              - λ7 × Multiple_Testing_Penalty
The score ranks research candidates. It does not estimate the probability of future profit and must never replace hard release gates.

--- TABLE ---

Deterministic execution | Same dataset snapshot, code commit, seed, and config produce identical results. | Fail CI and stop releases.

[Heading1] Part A  Product and architecture
[Heading2] 1  Goals and non goals
The platform exists to improve the quality and throughput of quantitative research while reducing the chance that apparent edge is caused by leakage, stale universes, optimistic fills, ignored costs, duplicated tests, or narrative bias. Profit is an outcome of genuine edge plus disciplined execution; it is not a software acceptance criterion that can be guaranteed before deployment.
[Heading3] Functional goals
•  Translate natural-language research ideas into a structured hypothesis with predicted mechanism, target universe, horizon, features, counterfactual, and falsification test.
•  Compile approved hypotheses into an Alpha DSL rather than unrestricted strategy code.
•  Replay the market event by event using only information available at each simulated decision time.
•  Simulate order lifecycle, partial fills, cancel latency, halts, price bands, short availability, fees, spread, impact, and end-of-day liquidation.
•  Apply walk-forward, purged and embargoed validation where labels overlap, regime tests, bootstraps, parameter perturbations, universe perturbations, factor attribution, and multiple-testing corrections.
•  Retain immutable lineage from source data through features, signals, orders, fills, PnL, conclusions, and deployment decisions.
•  Continuously compare simulated and realized slippage, fill rate, opportunity cost, exposure, and alpha decay.
[Heading3] Explicit non goals
•  Predicting individual stock movements from an LLM response or social-media narrative without a testable statistical specification.
•  Competing in microsecond market making, latency arbitrage, exchange colocation, or direct feed normalization in version 1.
•  Guaranteeing a positive day, month, Sharpe ratio, or cumulative PnL.
•  Automatically increasing leverage because a recent backtest or paper period is profitable.
•  Searching an unbounded parameter space and publishing only the best result.
•  Allowing the same process to modify strategy logic, approve its own evidence, and route live orders.

Qanat is a useful reference for inspectable table pipelines, point-in-time replay, MCP tooling, and net-of-cost reporting. [Heading1] Part B  Data and features
[Heading2] 6  Data principles
Intraday alpha research is primarily a data correctness problem. A strategy cannot be better than the timestamp semantics, corrections, corporate-action handling, universe history, quote quality, and execution assumptions beneath it. Data must be immutable at ingestion, versioned through normalization, and queryable as known at a specific decision time.
[Heading3] Required time fields
--- TABLE ---
Field | Meaning | Use
event_time | Timestamp assigned by the originating venue or source. | Market ordering and feature windows
receive_time | When the gateway received the message. | Latency and stale-feed detection
process_time | When normalization completed. | Pipeline monitoring
knowledge_time | Earliest time the system was legally and technically able to know the value. | Point-in-time joins and leakage prevention
effective_time | When a reference or corporate-action record becomes economically effective. | Adjustments and universe membership
revision_time | When a correction or restatement arrived. | As-was versus latest views
--- END TABLE ---
For live streams, event ordering must use sequence numbers where the vendor provides them. Late trades, corrections, and cancel/error messages must update derived bars without silently changing old experiment snapshots. Alpaca documentation, for example, exposes nanosecond timestamps, trade corrections, cancel/error events, updated bars, LULD bands, and trading-status messages; those are examples of the event types the canonical schema must preserve. [6]

[Heading2] 11  Universe construction
Universe selection is part of the strategy and must be point in time. A default research universe should contain U.S. listed common stocks and selected ETFs that pass price, median dollar volume, spread, trade count, corporate-action, and short-availability rules as of the prior session or as of the decision timestamp. Avoid using current index constituents in old periods.
[CodeBlock] universe:  asset_types: [common_stock, etf]  exchanges: [NYSE, NASDAQ, NYSE_ARCA]  min_price: 5.00  min_median_daily_dollar_volume_20d: 25_000_000  max_median_spread_bps_20d: 20  min_intraday_trade_count_20d: 5000  exclude:    - halted    - recent_ipo_under_60_sessions    - pending_delisting    - unresolved_corporate_action  membership_lag: 1_session
Thresholds are configurable research defaults, not claims of optimality. They must be varied in robustness tests, and capacity estimates must use participation limits and order-book conditions rather than average volume alone.
[Heading2] 12  Feature store
A feature is an immutable, versioned transformation with explicit input tables, availability rules, lookback, warm-up period, missing-value policy, and expected update cadence. Features may be composed only when their knowledge timestamps are less than or equal to the decision timestamp.

[Heading1] Part C  Alpha research and simulation
[Heading2] 13  Alpha DSL
The Alpha DSL is the contract between probabilistic research agents and deterministic trading infrastructure. It must be expressive enough for useful intraday research but narrow enough to validate, diff, version, mutate, and audit. Arbitrary agent-written Python may run only inside a sandboxed research branch and cannot become production strategy logic until represented by reviewed operators.
[CodeBlock] strategy:  id: intraday_momentum_volume_v1  family_id: intraday_momentum_volume  hypothesis: >    Five-minute residual momentum is more persistent when volume acceleration    and market breadth agree, except in high-spread or halt-adjacent states.clock:  timezone: America/New_York  evaluate: every_1m  first_entry: "09:45"  last_entry: "15:15"  flatten_by: "15:50"universe_ref: liquid_us_equities_v4features:  residual_return_5m: price.residual_return(window=5m, neutralize=sector)  volume_z: liquidity.volume_zscore(window=20d, time_of_day=true)  spread_bps: liquidity.nbbo_spread_bps()  breadth: market.equal_weight_return(window=5m)signal:  when: residual_return_5m > 1.0_sigma and volume_z > 1.5 and breadth > 0  score: rank(residual_return_5m) + 0.5 * rank(volume_z)  side: longorders:  entry: marketable_limit(offset_bps=3, ttl=3s)  exit:    - take_profit: 1.5_atr    - stop_loss: 0.8_atr    - time_stop: 25m    - portfolio_flatten: "15:50"risk:  risk_per_trade_nav_bps: 5  max_position_nav_pct: 2  max_symbol_adv_participation_pct: 0.10  max_concurrent_positions: 12
[Heading3] DSL validation rules
•  Every feature and operator resolves to a versioned registry entry.
•  Decision clock, feature availability lag, and order timestamp are explicit.
•  No expression may reference post-decision values, future extrema, final daily volume, or revised data unavailable at the time.
•  Entries, exits, and end-of-day behavior are complete; no ambiguous discretionary clause is allowed.
•  Order type, time in force, cancel/replace behavior, and price guard are explicit.
•  Risk values must be within the globally signed policy envelope; strategy files may tighten but never loosen them.
•  A compiler emits an operator graph, dependency manifest, static leakage report, complexity score, and canonical hash.

[Heading2] 15  Intraday strategy research catalog
The following are research templates, not recommended trades. Each must be tested as a family with full trial accounting and must compete against simple baselines. Familiar strategies often disappear after realistic spread, impact, latency, and selection controls.
--- TABLE ---

[Heading3] Research protocol for each family
1.  Pre-register the mechanism, target, decision clock, universe, core parameters, maximum search budget, expected failures, and acceptance criteria.
2.  Build a naive baseline before adding ML or alternative data. If complexity does not improve net out-of-sample utility, keep the baseline.
3.  Reserve a final untouched chronological holdout before examining results. Store its access event in the audit log.
4.  Run feature causality tests and label-overlap analysis before training.
5.  Use walk-forward training and validation with purging and embargo when samples share future return windows.
6.  Apply the critic battery and multiple-testing corrections to the entire strategy family, including abandoned variations.
7.  Advance to shadow and paper only after a written decision record explains the mechanism, evidence, weaknesses, capacity, and rollback triggers.
[Heading2] 16  Backtest engine
The backtester must be event driven. A bar-vectorized engine is acceptable for early feature research, but production eligibility requires an order-level replay at a resolution sufficient to model the intended holding period and order type. A strategy that trades on one-minute bars must not assume fills at the same bar close that triggered the signal.

[Heading2] 17  Fill and execution model
Use a hierarchy of simulators. Research may begin with conservative bar fills, but a candidate may advance only when evaluated with the model appropriate to its order style. A broker paper environment is necessary but insufficient: Alpaca explicitly notes that its paper system does not model market impact, information leakage, latency slippage, queue position, price improvement, regulatory fees, or dividends, and may ignore displayed quantity constraints. [5]
--- TABLE ---
Model | Use | Minimum assumptions
Bar pessimistic | Fast screening only | Next-bar availability; adverse side of spread; no fill if price merely touches under ambiguous path; volume participation cap.
Quote replay | Marketable and passive limit orders | NBBO at arrival; latency; crossing cost; displayed size; partial fills; stale quote rejection.
Trade and quote replay | Advanced candidate validation | Quote state, prints, trade conditions, queue estimate, cancels, corrections, venue status.
Broker paper | Integration and operational testing | Broker acknowledgments, rejects, partial fills, reconnects, duplicate prevention.
Live canary | Calibration only after approval | Minimal size; compare predicted versus realized fills and costs; no automatic scaling.
--- END TABLE ---
[Heading3] Cost decomposition
[CodeBlock] NetPnL = GrossMarkToMarket         - Commissions         - RegulatoryAndExchangeFees         - HalfSpreadOrEffectiveSpread         - MarketImpact         - LatencySlippage         - BorrowFeesAndRecallLoss         - Financing         - OpportunityCostOfMissedFills
•  Spread  Use the actual prevailing bid and ask at simulated arrival; do not use a constant basis-point deduction when quote data is available.
•  Impact  Estimate as a function of order notional, spread, volatility, order-book depth, and participation. Calibrate on realized canary executions.
•  Latency  Sample signal-to-submit and submit-to-ack distributions from production telemetry; rerun backtests under p50, p95, and stressed latency.
•  Borrow  Record availability and fee as known at entry. Reject a historical short if borrow plausibility is absent rather than assuming free inventory.
•  Fees  Version effective-dated broker, venue, exchange, and regulatory schedules. Recalculate net PnL when schedules change.
[Heading2] 18  PnL and attribution
PnL must reconcile from fills, not from desired weights. Mark realized and unrealized PnL separately, and attribute every dollar to alpha, beta, sector, spread, impact, fees, borrow, timing, and residual. Each position must answer why it exists and which signals, risk adjustments, and orders created it.
--- TABLE ---
Output | Required dimensions
Trade ledger | strategy, symbol, entry/exit, side, size, prices, fees, slippage, hold time, exit reason
Daily PnL | gross, cost components, net, realized, unrealized, long, short, benchmark residual
Exposure | gross, net, beta, sector, factor, liquidity bucket, concentration, short borrow
Execution | arrival price, decision price, fill price, effective spread, implementation shortfall, fill rate, cancel rate
Opportunity | submitted not filled, filtered by risk, suppressed by stale data, capacity limited

[Heading1] Part D  Validation portfolio and risk
[Heading2] 19  Anti overfitting architecture
The largest threat is an automated search process that runs thousands of related ideas and promotes the luckiest result. Bailey and collaborators formalize the probability of backtest overfitting and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio, which adjusts performance claims for selection bias, non-normality, and repeated trials. The platform must treat the full research path as part of the statistical evidence, not only the final strategy. [7][8]
[Small] Figure 3  Capital access is a sequence of revocable evidence gates
[Heading3] Experiment split design
•  Use chronological splits. Random shuffling is prohibited for time-dependent trading labels.
•  Maintain development, validation, and a final untouched test period. The final test is opened once per materially new family, not once per parameter setting.
•  For trained models, use rolling or expanding walk-forward folds. Train only on observations earlier than the validation fold.
•  Purge training samples whose label windows overlap the test fold and apply an embargo after the test fold when leakage can occur through nearby observations.
•  Fit scalers, imputers, feature selectors, regime boundaries, probability calibration, and hyperparameters inside each training fold.
•  Report aggregate results and the distribution across folds. A single favorable period is insufficient.
[Heading2] 20  Validation gauntlet
--- TABLE ---
Gate | Test | Default research threshold
Integrity | PIT audit, snapshot hash, causal-feature unit tests, full trial registry | All pass
Economic | Net OOS expectancy and PnL after base costs | Positive with uncertainty reported
Cost stress | Spread, slippage, fees, latency, and missed fills increased | Positive at 2x plausible costs; no catastrophic tail at 3x
Walk-forward | Performance across chronological folds | Majority of folds positive; no single fold dominates
Parameter stability | Neighboring parameters and simpler variants | Broad plateau; at least 70 percent of defined neighbors nonnegative
Universe stability | Liquidity cutoffs, sectors, symbols, and membership variants | No dependence on a tiny set of names
Concentration | Remove best day, week, and top contributors | Core conclusion survives
Bootstrap | Stationary or block bootstrap of daily or trade clusters | High probability of positive net expectancy; interval includes realistic uncertainty
Regime | Volatility, trend, dispersion, rates, event, and liquidity regimes | Failures understood and gated; no hidden catastrophic regime

Thresholds are starting governance defaults, not universal truths. They should be tightened or relaxed only through a documented policy change applied prospectively. A strategy cannot select the threshold after seeing results.
[Heading2] 21  Required robustness attacks
•  Shift every signal forward by one and two bars to expose accidental same-bar fills and fragile timing.
•  Delay submission by measured p50, p95, and outage-recovery latency.
•  Widen spread and multiply impact and fees independently rather than with one generic cost number.
•  Perturb lookbacks, thresholds, stop levels, holding periods, neutralization, universe cutoffs, and rebalance clocks on a predeclared neighborhood.
•  Remove the best year, month, week, day, stock, sector, and event category one at a time.
•  Use alternative but legitimate data cleaning rules and quote-condition filters.
•  Rebuild features from a second vendor sample where licensing permits.
•  Test on synthetic null labels and time-scrambled labels; the pipeline should not discover persistent edge.
•  Run placebo entry times and economically irrelevant features.
•  Apply capacity curves at increasing notional and participation rather than extrapolating small-size returns linearly.
•  Test kill-switch, halt, LULD, stale-feed, partial-fill, reject, disconnect, reconnect, and duplicate-event behavior inside replay.

[Heading2] 24  Portfolio construction
Accepted signals do not directly determine position sizes. A portfolio layer converts standardized forecasts into desired risk while accounting for forecast uncertainty, covariance, liquidity, capacity, transaction costs, existing holdings, and limits. Simpler allocation should be the default until evidence justifies complexity.
[CodeBlock] maximize_w    μᵀw - λrisk × wᵀΣw - λturn × Cost(w - w_prev) - λconc × Concentration(w)subject to    gross_exposure <= gross_limit              abs(net_exposure) <= net_limit              abs(position_i) <= min(position_cap_i, liquidity_cap_i, borrow_cap_i)              sector_exposure within bounds              predicted_expected_shortfall <= ES_budget              intraday_margin_usage <= broker_and_internal_limit
•  Standardize forecasts using training-only distributions and clip extremes.
•  Shrink expected returns toward zero as uncertainty, age, or live drift increases.
•  Use robust covariance or factor models; do not invert an unstable full covariance matrix without shrinkage.
•  Penalize turnover at the optimizer level so the agent cannot create gross alpha that disappears in execution.
•  Allocate across strategies based on marginal net utility and correlation, not standalone Sharpe alone.
[Heading2] 25  Risk policy
Risk is enforced in three independent places: strategy compilation, pre-trade checks, and continuous account monitoring. The broker may add stricter controls. The platform must discover the broker account rules rather than assuming the former pattern-day-trader framework: FINRA adopted new intraday margin standards effective June 4, 2026, eliminated the former day-trade-count designation and $25,000 minimum in the new rule, and allowed member firms to phase in implementation through October 20, 2027. [3]

Short selling must also respect locate availability, borrow economics, and Rule 201 restrictions after a 10 percent decline; the SEC notes that short positions can have theoretically unlimited loss. [11]
[Heading3] Pre trade checks
1.  Authenticate strategy version, account, operator state, and signed risk policy.
2.  Reject duplicate client order IDs and conflicting outstanding intents.
3.  Validate symbol status, trading session, halt state, LULD band, and fresh market data.
4.  Validate order side, type, quantity, notional, price collar, lot size, and time in force.
5.  For shorts, confirm broker shortability, estimated borrow cost, locate state where required, and Rule 201 handling.
6.  Project post-fill position, gross/net/sector/factor exposures, buying power, and intraday margin.
7.  Check strategy, symbol, account, daily-loss, rate, order-count, participation, and concentration limits.

The system should implement these protections even though the retail broker remains the regulated market-access gatekeeper. [14]

[Heading2] 29  Self improvement policy
The first self-improvement mechanism is procedural: better memory, fewer redundant experiments, stronger falsification, and improved calibration of costs and risk. Only after a large trustworthy experiment registry exists should the platform learn a research policy.
--- TABLE ---
State | Actions | Reward signal
Existing evidence, available data, research budget, regime coverage, failure clusters | Create feature, combine features, mutate DSL, run ablation, acquire dataset, stop family | Incremental out-of-sample net utility after robustness and diversity penalties
--- END TABLE ---
•  Use a contextual bandit before full reinforcement learning. The action is which experiment to run, not which stock to buy.
•  Compute reward only after validation, never from in-sample performance.
•  Charge the policy for compute, data, trial count, strategy complexity, and similarity to existing tests.
•  Preserve exploration with a fixed budget and do not let recent profitable families monopolize research.
•  Train and evaluate the research policy on historical registry slices so future outcomes remain unseen.
•  Keep the policy advisory until it outperforms simple experiment-selection heuristics prospectively.

[Heading3] Startup checklist
1.  Verify deployment identity, signed configuration, secrets source, clock synchronization, and trading date.
2.  Fetch broker capabilities and account restrictions, including the implemented intraday margin regime.
3.  Load broker orders, fills, positions, cash, and buying power; reconcile with the local event log.
4.  Connect market data, confirm sequence and freshness, and warm features without emitting orders.
5.  Validate calendar, session state, halts, LULD, borrow, risk limits, daily PnL baseline, and kill switch.

[Heading3] Shutdown checklist
1.  Block new risk and persist the reason and operator.
2.  Cancel outstanding entry orders and reconcile cancel outcomes.
3.  Flatten positions if policy requires, using price guards and halt-aware behavior; never assume a market order guarantees a safe price.
4.  Reconcile final fills, positions, cash, fees, and PnL with the broker.
5.  Seal the session event log, data-quality report, strategy versions, risk policy, incidents, and attribution report.

[Heading2] 33  Security and AI safety
•  Broker credentials exist only in the execution service secret store. Research and LLM services receive no credential, token, or network route capable of order submission.
•  Use separate paper and live accounts, keys, hosts, databases, queues, and alert channels. A configuration flag alone is insufficient separation.
•  Require least-privilege service identities, short-lived credentials where supported, network allowlists, encryption in transit and at rest, and audited secret rotation.
•  Sandbox research code with no broker network access, read-only snapshot mounts, resource quotas, and an explicit output directory.
•  Treat news, filings, web content, and retrieved documents as untrusted data. Strip active content and prevent prompt text from changing tool permissions.
•  Sign production strategy and risk-policy artifacts. The executor accepts only approved hashes from the release registry.
•  Maintain dependency locks, software bills of materials, vulnerability scanning, protected branches, mandatory review, and reproducible containers.
•  Exercise backup, restore, credential-revocation, broker-disconnect, and incident-response procedures.
NIST frames AI risk management as a voluntary way to incorporate trustworthiness into design, development, use, and evaluation; its Secure Software Development Framework recommends integrating security practices into the software lifecycle. Those principles support the platform separation, evidence, and release controls specified here. [9][10]
[Heading2] 34  Regulatory and operational constraints
This section is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Confirm requirements with the broker and a qualified professional before live deployment, especially if the system will trade for anyone else, accept outside capital, provide recommendations, or be sold as a service.
•  FINRA intraday margin rules changed in 2026, but brokers may be in different implementation phases through October 2027. Query and display the actual broker policy. [3]
•  Most covered U.S. securities transactions settle T+1 for trades on or after May 28, 2024. Cash-account and margin-account logic must use the broker-reported settled and available funds. [12]
•  Short-selling behavior must respect broker availability and Regulation SHO, including order marking, locate handling by the broker, and Rule 201 price restrictions. [11]
•  The simulator and live gateway must consume halt and LULD status. Price bands and pauses can prevent or delay execution. [13]
•  Market-data licenses govern storage, redistribution, user display, and derived data. Entitlements must be represented in the data catalog and access policy.
•  Retain complete records of strategy versions, approvals, orders, executions, reconciliations, incidents, and model changes for operational and tax reporting needs.
The SEC warns that day trading is highly risky, expensive, and capable of producing severe loss, especially with borrowed money. This is why the platform begins with research and paper modes, constrains leverage, and treats profitability as uncertain. [4]

[Heading2] 40  Acceptance criteria for version 1
•  A reviewer can reproduce any reported metric from the experiment ID alone using pinned data, code, container, config, and seed.
•  A static and dynamic causality test proves that features cannot access observations after their decision timestamp.
•  Every strategy variant appears in a family trial registry, including failed, canceled, and agent-generated experiments.
•  The simulator rejects same-bar look-ahead fills and reconciles cash, positions, and PnL exactly from fills.
•  Cost reports show gross, each cost component, base net, and stressed net results.
•  Known overfit synthetic strategies fail at least one validation gate; known stable synthetic signals pass within calibrated rates.
•  The agent cannot access live broker credentials, modify risk policies, open the final holdout silently, or approve a release.
•  Duplicate submission, network timeout, unknown order, disconnect, partial fill, halt, stale data, and kill-switch tests all resolve to documented safe states.
•  Paper and live environments cannot share account IDs or credentials and are visibly distinguished in the UI and logs.
•  No live order is possible before signed strategy, allocation, risk policy, operator approval, successful startup reconciliation, and fresh market data.
